# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

## Introduction

This document is the Privacy Policy for the **Wane English** application (hereinafter "the App"), developed by **[WANE ACADEMY]**. The App is designed to teach English to Persian and Kurdish speakers. We are committed to protecting our users' privacy, and this document transparently explains what information is collected and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1. Information We Do NOT Collect
We **do not collect any personal information**. Specifically:
- We do not store your name, email, phone number, or address
- There is no user account or registration process
- We do not read your device identifiers (IMEI, MAC Address, Advertising ID)
- We do not track your location (GPS)
- We do not use any analytics or behavioral tracking services

### 1.2. Locally Stored Information
For proper functionality, the following data is stored **only on your device** and never transmitted to our servers:
- **Selected language** (Persian or Kurdish)
- **Selected theme** (Light or Dark)
- **Learning progress** (words/sentences read per lesson)
- **Last position** in each section
- **Reminder settings**
- **Content cache** (for faster loading and offline use)

This data is completely removed when you uninstall the App.

### 1.3. Information Received From Server
The App fetches educational content (vocabulary, sentences, audio files, images, PDFs) from our servers. In this communication:
- A **fixed application token** is sent to verify the request originates from the official app
- Your **IP address** is logged by the server (standard web server behavior) — these logs are used only for security and debugging and are deleted shortly after
- No personally identifiable information is transmitted with these requests

## 2. Permissions We Request

| Permission | Purpose | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| **Internet** | Fetching educational content and audio playback | Yes |
| **Notifications** | Sending study reminders (optional - can be disabled in Settings) | No |
| **Network State** | Detecting VPN to display a warning and improve UX | Yes |

None of these permissions are used for advertising or commercial purposes.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Wane English App **uses no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services**. Specifically:

- **No AdMob** or any other ad network
- **No Firebase Analytics**, Google Analytics, or Mixpanel
- **No Facebook SDK**, AppsFlyer, or Adjust
- **No Crashlytics** or external crash reporting

All educational content is fetched exclusively from our own server (`waneacademy.com`).

## 4. Data Security

- **Server communication** uses HTTPS
- **Local data** is stored only in the app's private storage
- **Application token** is stored on our server and used only for request authentication

## 5. Children's Privacy

The Wane English App is designed for general use and is **suitable for children**. Since:
- We collect no personal information
- We display no advertisements
- There are no in-app purchases
- There is no way to contact adults or other users

The App is completely safe for children under 13 and complies with **COPPA** (US) and **GDPR-K** (EU).

## 6. User Rights

Since we retain no personal information, there is no data deletion request process needed. To clear all local data:
1. Inside the App: **Settings → Developer Tools → Full App Reset** (development builds only)
2. Or simply **uninstall** the App

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. For significant changes:
- The "Last updated" date will change
- Users will be notified via a new app version
