## Privacy Policy

Last updated: **October 12, 2026**

## Introduction

This document is the Privacy Policy for the **Wane Player** application (hereinafter referred to as "the App"), developed by **Sirvan Bodagh**. The App is designed to provide students with access to their purchased educational packages, including video lessons, audio files, text materials, and downloadable files.

As the developer, I respect your privacy and am committed to being transparent about the information the App handles and how it is used. This Privacy Policy explains what data I collect, why I collect it, and how you can manage it.

## 1. Information I Collect

### 1.1. Information You Provide

To use the App, you must log in with a **36-character access token** that is provided to you at the time of purchase. This token serves as your unique identifier in the App and is linked to your user account on my servers.

The following information is associated with your account on my servers:

- **Full name** (provided at the time of registration by the academy)
- **Mobile phone number**
- **Email address**
- **Purchased package IDs** (to determine which educational content you have access to)
- **Account status** (active/inactive)

The App itself does **not** collect additional personal information such as:
- Your physical address
- Your location (GPS)
- Your device identifiers such as IMEI or MAC address
- Your contacts, photos, or files on your device

### 1.2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide the App's features and improve user experience, the following data is stored **only on your device** and is not transmitted to my servers:

- **Access token** (encrypted in the device's private storage)
- **Device token** (a randomly generated unique identifier for this device)
- **User information** (name, mobile, package IDs — cached for offline display)
- **Selected language** (English, Persian, or Kurdish)
- **Selected theme** (Light or Dark)
- **Notification settings** (inactivity reminder preferences)
- **Notification schedule** (the next reminder time)
- **Content cache** (to improve loading speed and reduce data usage)

This locally stored data is removed when you uninstall the App. You can also clear this data using the **Full App Reset** option in Settings.

### 1.3. Information Received by the Server

The App connects to my servers to authenticate your account and retrieve educational content. During these requests:

- Your **access token** and **device token** are sent to verify your identity and ensure your account is only active on one device at a time. These two tokens are stored in my database for authentication purposes only.
- Your **IP address** may be recorded in standard server logs. These logs are used solely for security and troubleshooting purposes and are automatically deleted after a short period.
- Your **device model** and **operating system version** may be stored alongside your device token during login, solely to distinguish between different devices for authentication purposes.

I do **not** log, track, or store:
- Which packages, levels, or lessons you view
- Your learning progress or completion status
- Your location data
- Your contacts or calendar
- Your browsing history
- Any data from other apps on your device

The App does not send any analytics, behavioral tracking, or usage statistics to my servers. The only information stored in my database is the minimum data required for authentication: your access token and your device token.

## 2. Permissions I Request

| Permission | Purpose | Required? |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Internet** | Authenticating your account and retrieving educational content | Yes |
| **Notifications** | Sending optional inactivity reminders, which can be disabled in Settings | No |
| **Network State** | Detecting VPN usage to display a warning and improve the user experience | Yes |
| **Screen Capture Protection** | Preventing screenshots and screen recording of copyrighted educational content | Yes |
| **Keep Awake** | Preventing the device from sleeping during video playback | Yes |

I do not use these permissions for advertising or commercial tracking purposes.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Wane Player App **does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services**. Specifically, I do not integrate:

- **AdMob** or other advertising networks
- **Firebase Analytics**, Google Analytics, or Mixpanel
- **Facebook SDK**, AppsFlyer, or Adjust
- **Crashlytics** or external crash-reporting services

Educational content is retrieved from my own hosting server located at `waneacademy.com`. Standard network infrastructure and service providers involved in delivering the App's content may still process technical information, such as IP addresses, as part of normal operation.

## 4. Device Limitation Policy

To protect your account and prevent unauthorized sharing of your access token, the App enforces a **single-device login policy**:

- Each access token can only be active on **one device at a time**.
- When you log in on a new device, the previous device is automatically logged out.
- The previous device will show a warning message when it next opens the App, and you will be required to log in again.
- If your account has been inactive for more than **90 days**, you will be automatically logged out for security reasons.

This policy ensures that your purchased content remains private and is not shared with unauthorized users.

## 5. Data Security

I take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the App:

- **Server communication** is conducted over **HTTPS** to prevent eavesdropping.
- **Access tokens** are stored in the device's private storage and are never shared with third parties.
- **Device tokens** are randomly generated and cannot be used to identify you personally.
- **Server logs** are stored securely and are only accessible to me as the developer.
- **Content access** is validated on every request to ensure only authorized users can view paid content.

Although I take reasonable steps to protect data, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Wane Player App is designed for general educational use and may be suitable for children.

- The App does not require users to provide personal information directly.
- The App does not display advertisements.
- The App does not offer in-app purchases.
- The App does not provide features for users to communicate with strangers or other App users.

I have designed the App to minimize the handling of personal information. However, the App's suitability for children and its compliance with applicable laws, including the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** in the United States and applicable children's data protection requirements under the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** in the European Union, depend on the App's actual operation and applicable legal requirements.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

### 7.1. Request a Copy of Your Data
To request a copy of the personal information I have about you on my servers, please contact me using the information in Section 9. I will respond to your request within a reasonable time frame.

### 7.2. Request Account Deletion
To request deletion of your account and all associated data from my servers, please contact me directly using the information in Section 9. I will process your request within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements. Once your account is deleted, you will no longer be able to log in to the App.

### 7.3. Clear Local Data
To clear locally stored App data, you can:

1. Use **Settings → Full App Reset**, if this option is available in your App version.
2. **Uninstall the App** from your device.

Please note that uninstalling the App removes local data but does not automatically delete your account or server-side data. To delete your account, you must contact me directly as described in Section 7.2.

### 7.4. Disable Notifications
You can disable inactivity reminders at any time in **Settings → Notifications**.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or its data practices.

If I make significant changes:

- I will update the **Last updated** date at the top of this page.
- I will communicate significant changes through a new App version or another appropriate method, where applicable.

Continued use of the App after changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
