## Privacy Policy

Last updated: **October 12, 2026**

## Introduction

This document is the Privacy Policy for the **Wane Player** application (hereinafter referred to as "the App"), developed by **Sirvan Bodagh**. The App allows users with an existing Wane Player account to access educational content made available to them, including video lessons, audio files, and text materials.

As the developer, I respect your privacy and am committed to being transparent about the information the App handles and how it is used. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed, why it is needed, how it is protected, and what options are available to you.

## 1. Information I Collect and Process

### 1.1. Account Information

To use the App, you must sign in using a **36-character access token** associated with your Wane Player account. This token is used to authenticate your identity and determine whether your account is authorized to access the App's content.

The following information may be associated with your account on Wane Player's servers:

- **Full name**
- **Email address**
- **Content access information** (used to determine which educational materials are available to your account)
- **Account status** (active or inactive)

This information is maintained through the Wane Player system and is used by the App only as needed to authenticate your account and provide access to authorized educational content.

The App does not independently collect or request additional information such as:

- Your physical address
- Your GPS location
- Your contacts or calendar
- Your personal photos or files outside the App's own storage
- Device identifiers such as your IMEI or MAC address

### 1.2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide the App's features and improve your experience, the following information may be stored locally on your device:

- **Access token** (used to maintain your authenticated session)
- **Device token** (a randomly generated identifier used to associate your session with a device)
- **Cached account information** (such as your name and content access information, where needed for display)
- **Selected theme** (Light or Dark)
- **Notification settings** (including inactivity reminder preferences)
- **Notification schedule** (the next scheduled reminder time)
- **Content cache** (to improve loading speed and reduce data usage)

Locally stored information is removed when you uninstall the App, subject to your device's operating system and data-management behavior. You can also clear local App data using the **Full App Reset** option in Settings, if available in your version of the App.

### 1.3. Information Processed by the Server

The App communicates with my servers to authenticate your account, verify content access permissions, and retrieve educational materials.

During these requests:

- Your **access token** and **device token** are transmitted to authenticate requests and enforce the App's single-device login policy.
- Your **IP address** may be recorded in standard server logs for security, troubleshooting, and operational purposes. These logs are retained for a limited period according to the server's logging and retention practices.
- Your **device model** and **operating system version** may be stored alongside your device token to help distinguish between devices during authentication.

I do not intentionally collect detailed viewing histories or behavioral analytics through the App. The App is designed to retrieve authorized educational content without maintaining a detailed record of the lessons you watch or the materials you open.

Technical information necessary for authentication, account management, security, and content delivery may still be processed or retained as described in this policy.

## 2. Permissions I Request

| Permission | Purpose | Required? |

| **Internet** | Authenticating your account and retrieving educational content | Yes |
| **Notifications** | Sending optional inactivity reminders, which can be disabled in Settings | No |

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Wane Player App does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, or external crash-reporting SDKs. Specifically, I do not integrate:

- **AdMob** or other advertising networks
- **Firebase Analytics**, Google Analytics, or Mixpanel
- **Facebook SDK**, AppsFlyer, or Adjust
- **Crashlytics** or other third-party crash-reporting services

Educational content and account authentication are handled through my Wane Player hosting infrastructure at my secure hostin server.

However, standard network infrastructure and service providers involved in hosting or delivering the App's content may process technical information, such as IP addresses, as part of normal operation.

## 4. Device Limitation Policy

To protect your account and reduce unauthorized sharing of access credentials, the App enforces a **single-device login policy**:

- Each access token can be active on **one device at a time**.
- When you sign in on a new device, your previous device's session is invalidated.
- When the previous device next attempts to use the App, you may see a notification explaining that you need to sign in again.
- If your account remains inactive for more than **90 days**, the App may require you to sign in again for security reasons.

This policy helps protect your account and restrict access to authorized users.

## 5. Data Security

I take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the App:

- **Server communication** uses HTTPS to help protect data transmitted between the App and the server.
- **Locally stored information** is kept in the App's private storage, subject to the protections provided by the device's operating system.
- **Access tokens** are used to authenticate requests and determine whether access is authorized.
- **Device tokens** are used for device-session management and are not intended to identify you directly.
- **Content access** is validated through the server to help prevent unauthorized access to educational materials.

I also limit the information processed by the App to what is reasonably necessary for authentication, content delivery, and the App's core functionality.

Although I take reasonable steps to protect information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Wane Player App is designed for educational use and may be suitable for children, depending on how it is offered and used.

The App does not display advertisements, offer in-app purchases, or provide features for users to communicate with strangers or other App users.

However, the App uses an existing Wane Player account for authentication and may process account information, including a user's name and contact details, through the associated system.

I aim to minimize the information processed by the App and to protect users' privacy. The App's suitability for children and compliance with applicable laws, including the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** in the United States and applicable children's data protection requirements under the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** in the European Union, depend on its actual operation and the applicable legal requirements.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the following options regarding your information.

### 7.1. Request a Copy of Your Data

You may contact me to request information about the personal data associated with your account that I can access through the Wane Player system.

I will review and respond to your request within a reasonable time, subject to applicable legal requirements.

### 7.2. Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your Wane Player account and its associated personal information, please contact me using the email address provided in Section 9.

I will review your request and process it within 30 days, where applicable, subject to legal retention requirements and any necessary account-management procedures.

Deleting your account may remove your ability to sign in to the App and access the educational content associated with that account.

### 7.3. Clear Local App Data

To clear information stored locally on your device, you can:

1. Use **Settings → Full App Reset**, if this option is available in your App version.
2. **Uninstall the App** from your device.

Uninstalling the App removes its local data but does not automatically delete your Wane Player account or information retained on the server. To request account deletion, please follow the instructions in Section 7.2.

### 7.4. Disable Notifications

You can disable inactivity reminders at any time through **Settings → Notifications**, if this option is available in your App version.

You can also manage the App's notification permissions through your device's operating system settings.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its features, or its data-handling practices.

If I make significant changes:

- I will update the **Last updated** date at the top of this page.
- I will communicate significant changes through a new App version or another appropriate method, where applicable.

I encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how the App handles information.
